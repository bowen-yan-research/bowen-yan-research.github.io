> A biomimetic soft robotic system that integrates actuation, optical sensing, and perception for adaptive pipe inspection.

## Research question

How can mechanical design, soft actuation, and sensing be integrated and optimized for a pipe-inspection robot?

## Approach

The system combines mechanical structures, custom soft actuators, optical sensing hardware, and perception algorithms. Finite element analysis and system-level experiments are used to jointly optimize actuation and sensing parameters.

1. Integrate the mechanical structure and custom soft actuators.
2. Connect optical sensing hardware with perception algorithms.
3. Optimize actuation and sensing through simulation and experiments.

## Why it matters

The work brings hardware, sensing, and perception into one soft robotic system designed for adaptive pipe inspection.
