> A learner-centric perspective on embodied data, centered on how effectively the data supports policy learning.

## Research question

How can we assess embodied data in terms of its usefulness to the learning policy?

## Approach

This work introduces Learning Effectiveness of Data (LED), a learner-centric metric for predicting policy-learning effectiveness. LED is used to guide efficient embodied data curation, connecting dataset selection with the needs of the learner.

1. Assess data from the perspective of policy learning.
2. Use LED to predict learning effectiveness.
3. Guide data curation toward more efficient policy learning.

## Why it matters

The objective is to support stronger policy learning with less data by focusing on how useful that data is to the learner.
