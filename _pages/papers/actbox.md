**Under review at ICASSP 2027**

**Bowen Yan**, Chunyi Li, and Guangtao Zhai.

> A study of spatial communication formats for vision-language models that understand a scene and directly generate task-control actions.

## Research question

How does the way we communicate spatial information affect a vision-language model’s understanding and control decisions?

## Approach

ActBox investigates visual boxes, image grids, and textual coordinates as spatial communication modalities. It examines how these representations support contextual understanding and the accuracy of directly generated task-control actions.

1. Represent spatial information through visual boxes, image grids, or textual coordinates.
2. Use these representations to support VLM contextual understanding.
3. Study their effect on directly generated task-control actions.

## Why it matters

The work investigates the interface between spatial understanding and action generation, with the aim of making model-generated control more accurate.
